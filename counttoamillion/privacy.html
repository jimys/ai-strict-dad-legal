隐私政策

生效日期：2026年11月19日

《数到一百万》（Count To A Million，以下简称"本游戏"）尊重并保护您的隐私。本游戏不收集任何个人信息。

1. 麦克风

  • 本游戏用声音来玩：您大声读出数字，游戏通过麦克风听到后判断是否读对。
  • 语音识别完全在您的设备上离线进行。声音只在内存中实时处理，不会被录音、保存或上传，也不会发送给我们或任何第三方。
  • 您可以随时在系统「设置」中关闭本游戏的麦克风权限。

2. 我们不收集的信息

  • 本游戏没有账号系统，不要求您提供姓名、邮箱、电话等任何个人信息。
  • 本游戏不包含广告，不使用任何第三方统计、追踪或广告 SDK。
  • 本游戏不访问您的位置、通讯录、相册或摄像头。

3. 保存在您设备上的数据

  • 数到第几、计时、语言和音效设置保存在您的设备本地，不会上传到我们的服务器（我们没有服务器）。

4. 网络与付费

本游戏是一次买断的付费游戏，没有内购、没有广告。

游戏本身不连接网络。唯一的例外是可选的 Apple Game Center 排行榜：如果您登录了 Game Center，您的最高计数和数到一百万的用时会通过 Apple 的 Game Center 服务提交到排行榜，并按您的 Game Center 设置向其他玩家显示。这些数据由 Apple 按照 Apple 的隐私政策处理，我们不会收到或保存。您可以在系统「设置 > Game Center」中退出登录。

5. 儿童隐私

本游戏不会有意收集任何儿童的信息。

6. 数据删除

卸载本游戏即可删除所有保存在设备上的数据。

7. 政策更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策将在此页面发布，并更新生效日期。

8. 联系我们

如有任何隐私相关问题，请联系：funcitysayhello@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Count To A Million ("the Game") respects your privacy. The Game does not collect any personal information.

1. Microphone

  • You play the Game with your voice: you say numbers out loud and the Game listens through the microphone to check them.
  • Speech recognition runs entirely offline on your device. Audio is processed in memory in real time; it is never recorded, stored or uploaded, and never sent to us or to any third party.
  • You can turn off the Game's microphone access at any time in your device's Settings.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number or any other personal information.
  • The Game contains no ads and uses no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • The Game does not access your location, contacts, photos or camera.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Your count, counting time, language and sound settings are stored on your device and are never uploaded to us (we do not run any servers).

4. Network and Payments

The Game is a one-time paid purchase, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

The Game itself does not connect to the internet. The only exception is the optional Apple Game Center leaderboards: if you are signed in to Game Center, your highest count and your time to reach one million are submitted through Apple's Game Center service and shown to other players according to your Game Center settings. Apple handles this data under Apple's privacy policy; we never receive or store it. You can sign out in Settings > Game Center.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Deleting Your Data

Uninstalling the Game deletes all data stored on your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updated policies will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact: funcitysayhello@gmail.com
