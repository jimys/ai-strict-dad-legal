隐私政策

生效日期：2026年6月1日

Glow Up Instantly（以下简称“本应用”）尊重并保护您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

1. 信息收集

本应用可能收集或处理以下信息：

  • 照片数据：您主动选择上传或拍摄的参考图片，仅用于生成您请求的 AI 图像效果。
  • 生成指令：您选择的预设、风格、模型、提示词以及完成生成所需的技术请求数据。
  • 生成结果：AI 生成的图片会显示在应用内，并可由您保存到设备相册。
  • 购买信息：通过 Apple StoreKit 处理订阅和应用内购买，我们不会直接访问您的支付卡号或 Apple ID 密码。
  • 设备权限：相册权限仅用于选择、保存图片；通知或震动仅用于生成完成提醒。
  • 诊断信息：Apple 可能向开发者提供匿名崩溃或性能数据，用于改进应用稳定性。

2. 信息使用

我们仅将信息用于：

  • 提供 AI 图片生成、预览、保存和分享功能。
  • 处理订阅、点数购买、恢复购买和会员权益。
  • 维护应用安全、排查错误并改进用户体验。

3. AI 图片处理

为了完成图片生成请求，只有在您在应用内点击同意并开始生成后，您选择的参考图片、生成指令、提示词、模型/预设选择以及完成请求所需的技术数据，才会被发送至第三方 AI 服务进行处理。

根据您选择的生成模型，请求可能会发送给火山引擎豆包 Seedream 和/或 fal.ai。这些服务商仅用于处理您主动请求的 AI 图片生成，并将生成结果返回给本应用。

我们要求第三方 AI 服务商对其处理的数据提供与本隐私政策相同或同等水平的保护，并仅为提供图片生成服务而处理相关数据。请不要上传您无权使用的图片，或包含敏感个人信息、违法内容的图片。

4. 数据存储与安全

  • 生成结果默认保存在应用会话中，您可以选择保存到系统相册。
  • 应用设置和点数状态会保存在设备本地或通过 Apple 购买凭证恢复。
  • 我们采取合理措施保护数据，但互联网传输和电子存储无法保证绝对安全。

5. 第三方服务

本应用可能使用以下第三方服务：

  • Apple StoreKit：用于处理订阅、应用内购买和恢复购买。
  • Apple Photos Framework：用于选择和保存图片。
  • 火山引擎豆包 Seedream：用于处理您主动提交的图片生成请求。
  • fal.ai：用于处理您主动提交的图片生成请求，包括部分图像生成、编辑或视觉预处理模型。

Apple 服务受 Apple 隐私政策 约束。

6. 数据删除

您可以删除应用生成并保存到相册中的图片，或卸载本应用以删除本地应用数据。如需与隐私相关的帮助，请通过下方联系方式联系我们。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

8. 政策更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策将在此页面发布，并更新生效日期。

9. 联系我们

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2026

Glow Up Instantly ("the App") respects and protects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Photo data: Reference images you choose to upload or capture are used only to generate the AI image results you request.
  • Generation instructions: The preset, style, model, prompt text, and technical request data needed to complete the generation you request.
  • Generated results: AI-generated images are shown in the App and may be saved by you to your device photo library.
  • Purchase information: Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed through Apple StoreKit. We do not directly access your payment card number or Apple ID password.
  • Device permissions: Photo library access is used for selecting and saving images; notifications or vibration may be used to alert you when generation is complete.
  • Diagnostics: Apple may provide anonymous crash or performance information to help improve app stability.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide AI image generation, preview, saving, and sharing features.
  • Process subscriptions, credit purchases, purchase restoration, and premium benefits.
  • Maintain app security, troubleshoot issues, and improve the user experience.

3. AI Image Processing

To fulfill image generation requests, only after you consent in the App and start generation, the reference image you select, generation instructions, prompt text, model/preset selection, and technical request data needed to complete the request may be sent to third-party AI services for processing.

Depending on the generation model you choose, requests may be sent to Volcengine Doubao Seedream and/or fal.ai. These providers are used only to process the AI image generation you request and return generated results to the App.

We require third-party AI providers that process this data to provide the same or equal protection described in this Privacy Policy and to process the data only for providing the requested image generation service. Please do not upload images you do not have the right to use, or images containing sensitive personal information or unlawful content.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Generated results are displayed in the App session, and you may choose to save them to the system photo library.
  • App settings and credit status may be stored locally on your device or restored through Apple purchase receipts.
  • We use reasonable safeguards to protect data, but no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit: For subscriptions, in-app purchases, and purchase restoration.
  • Apple Photos Framework: For selecting and saving images.
  • Volcengine Doubao Seedream: For processing image generation requests you submit.
  • fal.ai: For processing image generation requests you submit, including certain image generation, image editing, or visual preprocessing models.

Apple services are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete generated images saved to your photo library, or uninstall the App to remove local app data. For privacy-related assistance, contact us using the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated policies will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
