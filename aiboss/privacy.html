隐私政策

生效日期：2026年11月14日

《成为AI公司老板》（以下简称"本游戏"）尊重并保护您的隐私。本游戏不收集任何个人信息。

1. 我们不收集的信息

  • 本游戏没有账号系统，不要求您提供姓名、邮箱、电话等任何个人信息。
  • 本游戏不包含广告，不使用任何第三方统计、追踪或广告 SDK。
  • 本游戏不访问您的位置、通讯录、相册、麦克风或摄像头。

2. 保存在您设备上的数据

  • 游戏进度、存档、语言和画面设置只保存在您的设备本地，不会上传到我们的服务器（我们没有服务器）。
  • 角色配音由设备本地的语音合成生成，台词文字不会发送到任何网络服务。

3. 应用内购买

"完整版"解锁通过 Apple 的 App Store 应用内购买完成。付款由 Apple 处理，我们收不到也不保存您的任何支付信息。购买记录由 Apple 管理，您可以随时在游戏里"恢复购买"。

4. 儿童隐私

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集任何儿童的信息。

5. 数据删除

卸载本游戏即可删除所有保存在设备上的数据。

6. 政策更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策将在此页面发布，并更新生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Become an AI Company Boss ("the Game") respects your privacy. The Game does not collect any personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number or any other personal information.
  • The Game contains no ads and uses no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • The Game does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Game progress, saves, language and display settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded (we do not run any servers).
  • Character voices are generated by on-device speech synthesis; dialogue text is never sent to any online service.

3. In-App Purchases

The "Full Game" unlock is an Apple App Store in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive or store any of your payment information. Apple keeps the purchase record, and you can use "Restore Purchase" in the Game at any time.

4. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Deleting Your Data

Uninstalling the Game deletes all data stored on your device.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updated policies will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact: funcitysayhello@gmail.com
